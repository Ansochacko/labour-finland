Before you arrive

What should I prepare before moving?

Use this as a research map. Required documents and permissions depend on your nationality, purpose and circumstances.

Check current requirements with the relevant Finnish authority before applying or travelling. Labour Finland does not decide eligibility.

Reason for moving

Study, work, family or another purpose can lead to different official routes.

Visas & permits →

Documents

Make a list of identity, education, employment and family documents you already have. Confirm what is currently required from the authority that handles your route.

Residence or visa pathway

The correct route depends on nationality, purpose, duration and other circumstances.

Check current requirements on Migri →

Budget planning

List likely categories such as travel, housing, food, transport, insurance and setup costs. Labour Finland does not publish unsourced cost-of-living figures.

Accommodation

Research housing options for the place you expect to live. Availability and rules vary by city and housing type.

Places in Finland →

Weather and clothing

Finland has distinct seasons. Pack for the time of year you will arrive and plan to add items after you can see local conditions.

Transport

Look up how you will travel from your arrival point to housing, studies or work. Local tickets and cards differ by region.

Banking and payments

Opening an account and using payment methods can depend on identification and residence details. Confirm current steps with the bank or service you intend to use.

Healthcare and medicines

Do not rely on a general website for medical or eligibility decisions. Ask a healthcare professional about medicines you need to bring and confirm coverage with the relevant authority.

Study or work preparation

If you already have a place to study or a job offer, keep copies of those documents and review stated pay against verified wage information where available.
