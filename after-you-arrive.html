After you arrive

What should I do during my first weeks?

Required actions depend on your situation. This is a map of topics people often need to check, not a universal checklist.

Registration and identity

Some people need to register with Finnish authorities after arriving. Confirm whether this applies to you.

Tax practicalities

If you will work in Finland, tax setup is often handled through the Finnish Tax Administration.

Vero →

Banking and phone

Bank accounts and mobile connections can depend on identification and address details. Ask the provider what they currently require.

Housing and transport

Confirm your address, building rules and local tickets. Services differ by municipality.

Healthcare and insurance

Access can depend on residence, employment, studies and other circumstances. Confirm current information with the relevant authority or your institution.

Employment, contracts and payslips

Read your contract, keep payslips and compare stated pay with verified wage information where Labour Finland has published it.

Language

Finnish and Swedish are national languages. English is widely used in some workplaces and studies, but this varies.

Everyday services

Groceries, libraries and local services are practical next steps once housing is in place.
