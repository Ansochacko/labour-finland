Scope of information

Disclaimer

Labour Finland provides general informational content about wages and working life in Finland. Information can change. Confirm current information with applicable agreements, employers, organisations and authorities before relying on it for a decision.

Labour Finland does not provide individualized legal advice, tax advice, immigration advice or employment advice.

The website is about Finland. People outside Finland may read it, but Finnish wage, working-life or immigration information should not be assumed to apply in another country.

Calculator estimates

Calculator results are mathematical gross-pay estimates based only on the values entered. They are not a legal entitlement, an official salary, a tax calculation or a take-home-pay figure. They do not include taxation or terms that have not been entered and verified.

Independence

Labour Finland is an independent information service and is not affiliated with or operated by the Finnish government, any public authority, trade union, employer organisation or recruitment agency. It is not Migri, Vero, Statistics Finland, a municipality or a recruitment agency.
