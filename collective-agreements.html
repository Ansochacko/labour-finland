Collective agreements

In many Finnish jobs, minimum employment terms can come from an applicable collective agreement rather than from one national euro minimum wage.

A collective agreement is a negotiated set of employment terms for a sector or group of work. It can affect pay scales, working time and other conditions. Whether a particular agreement applies depends on the work and the employment relationship. Labour Finland does not determine that for you.

This page lists only agreements that exist in the Labour Finland dataset. It does not reproduce complete agreement text.
