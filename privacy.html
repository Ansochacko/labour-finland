Privacy

Privacy

This page describes the website as it is actually built today.

Labour Finland is a static information website. It does not currently include analytics scripts, advertising scripts, user accounts or a contact form that collects messages. This page describes the site as it is actually built. It does not claim that no personal data is processed.

Website operator

The public operator identity, a contact address, and the data-controller details for Labour Finland will be published here before the public launch of LabourFinland.com. Until those details are published, privacy requests cannot be received through this website.

Information that may be processed

When you visit the site, the hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time of request. That processing depends on the host you actually use and is not performed by scripts in these pages. After a host is chosen, this page should be checked against that host's processing.

Cookies and local storage

Labour Finland's own scripts do not set analytics cookies and do not use localStorage or sessionStorage. There is therefore no non-essential tracking cookie banner in this version. A consent tool will be added only if non-essential tracking or advertising is introduced, and scripts that need consent will not run before that choice.

What is not installed

No analytics, advertising, social-media pixels, tag managers, captcha, newsletter tools or embedded third-party fonts are installed in this version. If they are added later, this page and any required choices will be updated first.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other organisations, including Finnish authorities and PAM. Those sites are not operated by Labour Finland and have their own privacy practices.

Contact and corrections

The contact page does not currently collect or store messages. A public contact method is required before launch so that correction and privacy requests can be received.

Privacy choices

There are no optional analytics or advertising cookies to accept or reject in this version. A privacy-choice control will be provided if optional tracking is introduced.

No optional analytics or advertising technologies are used in this version.
